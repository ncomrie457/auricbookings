**To:** hello@elliosa.com
**From:** auricmovement@outlook.com
**Subject:** Your socks sold themselves at our sold-out Nov 22 class

> Leads with proof, not with the ask — and the proof is about THEIR
> product, not the category. "I bought your socks with my own money,
> my guests loved them, and they're going back for more" is the single
> strongest thing a brand can be told by a stranger. Everything else in

---

Hi there,

I was pointed your way by the Elliosa team — they said partnerships
are handled here, so I hope this is the right door.

I'm Niara, founder of Auric Movement. We host curated Pilates events
across New York — reformer classes at Valley Studio in West Hempstead
and Maison Luxe in Brooklyn, plus mat and wellness events in the city.
Small rooms on purpose: six people per reformer session, two to four
sessions a date. Our last three reformer dates sold out.

Here's why I'm writing: you already worked in my room, and you didn't
know about it.

On November 22 we ran a sold-out Friendsgiving reformer class at
Valley Studio. I bought Elliosa socks for every guest, out of my own
pocket, and put a pair in each goodie bag. They were the most
talked-about thing in the bag — and several guests have since told me
they're going back to buy their own. I've put a lot of brands in those
bags. That reaction is rare, and it was yours.

You can see the room that morning here:
book.auricmovement.com/turkey-photos

It makes sense when you look at the room: grip socks are *required* at
our reformer studios. It's in every confirmation and reminder email we
send, and guests who arrive without a pair have to buy them at the
front desk before they can get on the machine. So a pair in the bag
isn't a sample that ends up in a drawer — it's worn that morning,
photographed in the room, and used every week after.

So I'd like to keep putting Elliosa in those bags — for more than one
room at a time, which is more than I can fund myself. Would you be
open to sending a run for an upcoming date?

**What partners get from us:**

- Product in the hand of every guest, opened in the room, not mailed out
- A named credit on the event's page at book.auricmovement.com, plus a
  card in our Featured Partners carousel with a photo and a link to you
- Named, linked, and — if you'd like — a discount code of yours, in the
  post-event thank-you email that goes to every attendee
- Tagged across our Instagram posts and stories from the event, and the
  recap that follows

Previous partners include LMNT, Vital Proteins, Topicals, Lemon
Perfect, Nuun, Grown Alchemist, Beekeeper's Naturals, Grüns and MASA
Chips, so this is a well-worn path for us. And our guests notice the
bags without being asked — this one went up on Google this week:

> *"Niara's excellent instruction, positive energy, and generosity with
> thoughtful goodie bags make the class extra special."*

Happy to start with a single date so you can see the photos and the
response before committing to anything further. I can send our calendar,
guest numbers and past recaps whenever useful.

Thank you for your time — I'd love to have you in the room.

Warmly,
Niara

## Check before you send

- **Were they grip socks?** The "required at our studios" paragraph
  turns on it. If what you bought were their regular socks, cut that
  paragraph — the proof above it stands on its own and does not need
  the argument.
- **"Our last three reformer dates sold out"** — Sep 13, Nov 21 and
  Nov 22 are all marked sold out on the site. Just confirm that's the
  claim you want in writing.
- **A photo of the bag with the socks in it would be worth more than
  the rest of this email.** The recap page shows the room, the people
  and the food table, but no close-up of a goodie bag, so the email
  only claims the room. If you have one on your phone, attach it. If
  not, a leftover pair photographed next to a bag and a reformer takes
  two minutes and does the same job — a brand wants to see their
  category sitting in the thing you're describing.
