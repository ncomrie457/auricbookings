## Check before you send

- **Do they actually make grip socks?** The whole middle of this email
  turns on it. If Elliosa makes regular socks rather than grip socks,
  cut that paragraph and lead with the goodie bag instead — the
  "required equipment" argument doesn't hold and a brand will know it.
- **"Our last three reformer dates sold out"** — Sep 13, Nov 21 and Nov
  22 are all marked sold out on the site. Check that's the claim you
  want to make before it goes out in writing.
- **"2027 season"** — change to whichever dates you actually want them
  for. Vague is weaker than "March 14 and April 18".
- **Attach or link one recap.** book.auricmovement.com/turkey-photos
  shows a real room with real people and real goodie bags. A brand
  deciding whether to ship you product will look at that before they
  read anything else.
