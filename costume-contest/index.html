Auric Movement

Best Costume Contest
Official Rules

Shhh… Quiet on the Creek · Sat, October 24, 2026 · Valley Studio, West Hempstead NY
JUDGED ON THE COSTUME, NOT AT RANDOM.
Entry is included with your class — there is nothing extra to buy.

1. Sponsor

This contest (the "Contest") is run by Auric Movement LLC, New York, NY. Questions: auricmovement@outlook.com.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years or older at the time of entry. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate family are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. When

This Contest runs at Shhh… Quiet on the Creek, Saturday 24 October 2026 at Valley Studio, across all three sessions — 11:30 AM, 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM. Everyone in costume at any of the three sessions is entered, and one winner is chosen for the day.

This Contest is separate from our Fall 2026 Valley Studio prize drawing, which has its own Official Rules and is decided at random rather than judged.

4. How to Enter

Turn up to your booked session in costume. That is the whole entry — there is no form, no slip, and nothing extra to pay. Entry is included with the class you already booked.

All three sessions are judged together, so it makes no difference which one you book. Costumes must be safe to move in on a reformer: nothing trailing, nothing that blocks your vision, nothing that could catch in the springs or carriage. The instructor may ask you to remove or adjust part of a costume for safety, and doing so does not affect your entry.

Keep it kind. Costumes that mock or caricature a race, ethnicity, religion, disability or gender identity are not eligible, in line with our Code of Conduct.

5. Prize

One (1) 4-class package at Valley Studio, awarded once for the day. As part of the prize, the winner agrees to book a different instructor for each of the four classes. The prize is non-transferable, has no cash value, cannot be substituted or exchanged, and must be used within two (2) months of being awarded. Approximate retail value: $160 (subject to Valley Studio's then-current rates and availability).

6. How the Winner Is Chosen

The Sponsor judges every costume worn across the three sessions and names one winner for the day. Judging is on effort, originality, and how well the costume suits moving on a reformer. This is a decision of skill, not chance.

The winner is decided after the final session and told by email within 48 hours. You do not need to stay for the announcement — attending your own session in costume is all that is required. If a winner cannot be reached within seven (7) days, or declines, the Sponsor may name another entrant. The Sponsor's decision is final.

7. General Conditions

By entering, you agree to these Official Rules and to the Sponsor's decisions, which are final and binding on all matters relating to the Contest. The Sponsor may disqualify any entry it believes to be unsafe, fraudulent, or in violation of these rules, and may cancel or amend the Contest if it cannot be run as planned.

8. Photography

Winners may be photographed with their prize for Auric Movement's own promotion. If you would rather not be photographed, tell us at the event and that will be respected — it does not affect your entry or your prize.

9. Privacy

Information you provide is used only to administer the Contest and is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. It is not sold or shared for marketing.

10. Not Sponsored by Third Parties

This Contest is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Valley Studio, or any other third party. Valley Studio is the prize venue only.
