Why 48 Hours

These are intimate sessions — a small group, five reformers, and a waitlist of people hoping for one of them.

That’s what makes the room feel the way it does, and it’s also why one empty reformer is felt. Two days’ notice is enough time for me to reach someone on that waitlist and get them booked, waivered and ready. Less than that, and the spot usually just goes unused. A quick heads-up is the whole difference.

I know life happens — plans shift, work runs long, bodies have their own ideas. So I try to be as fair as I possibly can about it. Here’s exactly how it works:

Cancellation, Credit & Transfer

Cancel 48+ hours ahead → you get a credit.
Email me at least 48 hours before your session start time and I’ll issue you a credit for the full amount you paid, good toward any future Auric Movement class or event of your choosing hosted by me. The credit is valid for 6 months from the day it’s issued.

Bring a friend in your place instead.
Spot transfers are welcome up to 24 hours before the event. Just email me both names and emails and I’ll move the reservation over — they’ll get their own confirmation and waiver.

Inside 48 hours, or a no-show.
These aren’t eligible for a credit — by then the spot can’t realistically be filled. I do hold to this, and it’s the policy you agreed to at booking. All sales are final; there are no cash refunds either way.

For Saturday the 26th, that 48-hour mark lands on Thursday, September 24th — at whatever time your session starts.
