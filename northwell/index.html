This Professional Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of the date signed below by Auric Movement LLC, a New York limited liability company (“Provider”), and Northwell Health, on behalf of Lenox Hill Hospital, Office of Academic Affairs (“Client”). Client engages Provider to lead one or more Pilates instruction events for its personnel.

1. Engagement & Services

Client engages Provider to plan and personally lead Pilates and related wellness instruction events (the “Services”) for Client’s faculty, house staff, trainees, employees, and invited guests (“Participants”), as described in Exhibit A. Provider is the instructor who will deliver the Services. Provider will supply any specialized equipment Provider elects to use; Client will provide reasonable venue access and confirm Participant counts at least three business days before each event.

2. Term

This Agreement runs from the Effective Date until completion of the Services, unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

3. Fees & Payment

Flat rates: Mat Pilates (50 min) — $275 per class; Reformer Pilates — $350 per class. Fees include instructor time, standard setup and teardown, and Participant waivers, and exclude venue rental and pre-approved travel or materials. Provider invoices per event or monthly; undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days. Venue rental is not included — if Client requires Provider to book the venue, Client pays the venue directly or reimburses Provider at cost, in addition to the class fee.

4. Independent Contractor

Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee or agent of Client, and is responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and compliance.

5. Instructor Qualifications

Provider is a qualified Pilates instructor, appropriately trained and certified where applicable. Any substitute or assistant instructor will meet the same standards, and Provider remains responsible for them. While on Client premises, Provider will comply with Client’s reasonable policies and complete any vendor credentialing Client reasonably requires.

6. Insurance

Provider will maintain Commercial General Liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate, professional/participant liability, and Workers’ Compensation as required by law. On Client’s request, Provider will furnish a certificate of insurance and, if permitted by its policy, name Northwell Health and Lenox Hill Hospital as additional insureds; if its policy cannot accommodate that, the Parties will discuss a reasonable alternative.

7. Participant Health & Waivers

The Services involve physical exercise. Provider will have each Participant complete a liability waiver and assumption-of-risk acknowledgment before participating and will conduct the Services safely and professionally.

8. Indemnification

Each Party will indemnify the other (and, for Provider, Northwell Health and Lenox Hill Hospital) from third-party claims to the extent arising from its own negligence, willful misconduct, or breach.

9. Limitation of Liability

Except for indemnification, confidentiality breaches, or gross negligence/willful misconduct, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and each Party’s aggregate liability is capped at the total fees under Exhibit A.

10. Compliance & Confidentiality

Each Party complies with applicable law. The Services are not expected to involve protected health information (PHI); if PHI access becomes necessary, the Parties will first execute a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. Each Party keeps the other’s non-public information confidential.

11. Use of Name & Publicity

Neither Party may use the other’s name, logo, or marks — including “Northwell Health” or “Lenox Hill Hospital” — in marketing without prior written consent. Nothing here implies Client’s endorsement of Provider.

12. Cancellation

Either Party may cancel or reschedule an event with at least seven (7) days’ written notice at no charge; Client cancellation with less notice incurs 50% of the affected event fee. Weather/venue issues outside either Party’s control are rescheduled without penalty.

13. Termination

Either Party may terminate on thirty (30) days’ written notice, or immediately for an uncured material breach after ten (10) days’ notice.

14. General

Force majeure applies to events beyond a Party’s reasonable control. Neither Party may assign without consent. This Agreement is governed by New York law, venue in New York County. It is the entire agreement between the Parties, may be amended only in a signed writing, and may be executed electronically and in counterparts, each an original.

Exhibit A — Scope, Schedule & Fees

Event(s), date(s), venue, and Participant counts to be confirmed in writing by the Parties. Fees per Section 3: Mat $275 / Reformer $350 per class; venue rental billed separately if Provider books it.
