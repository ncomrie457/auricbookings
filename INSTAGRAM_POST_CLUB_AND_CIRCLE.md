# Instagram — the Auric Club + the Auric Circle

Two things, two posts. Putting both in one post splits the ask: "check
your marks" and "go support these women" are different actions, and a
caption that asks for two gets neither. Post them a few days apart,
Circle first — it gives before it asks, which earns the Club post its
attention.

Everything below is ready to copy. Nothing in it is a claim I invented:
the Club numbers are the ones on your Members tab, and the businesses
are the seven in the Circle right now.

  shout-out; everybody minds finding out about it from a friend.
