Valley Studio Raffle
Official Rules

Fall 2026 attendee raffle · Valley Studio, West Hempstead NY
NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN.
A purchase or payment will not improve your chances of winning.

1. Sponsor

This raffle (the "Raffle") is run by Auric Movement LLC, New York, NY. Questions: auricmovement@outlook.com.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years or older at the time of entry. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate family are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Promotion Period

The Raffle runs in connection with Auric Movement's Fall 2026 reformer events held at Valley Studio: Riddim & Kompa (Sun, Sept 13), Shhh… Quiet on the Creek (Sat, Oct 24), and Turkey Burn! (Sat, Nov 21). A separate winner may be drawn for each event.

4. How to Enter

There are two ways to enter, each giving one (1) entry with equal chances of winning:

  1. Attend a class: Attend an Auric Movement class at Valley Studio during the Promotion Period. You'll receive one raffle ticket at the event.
  2. Free entry (no purchase necessary): Email auricmovement@outlook.com — or send a direct message to @auricmovement on Instagram — with your full name and the event you'd like to be entered for, before that event's drawing. Limit one free entry per person per event.

Both methods have the same odds of winning. No purchase or payment is required, and a purchase will not increase your chances.

5. Prize

One (1) 4-class package at Valley Studio per drawing. As part of the prize, the winner agrees to book a different instructor for each of the four classes. The prize is non-transferable, has no cash value, cannot be substituted or exchanged, and must be used within three (3) months of being awarded. Approximate retail value: $160 (subject to Valley Studio's then-current rates and availability).

6. Drawing & Notification

The winner for each event will be selected at random from all eligible entries and announced at that event. Odds of winning depend on the total number of entries received. If a winner is not present, the Sponsor will make reasonable efforts to notify them by email or Instagram within 7 days; if the winner cannot be reached within 14 days, an alternate may be drawn.

7. General Conditions

By entering, you agree to these Official Rules and to the Sponsor's decisions, which are final and binding on all matters relating to the Raffle. The Sponsor may disqualify any entry it believes to be fraudulent or in violation of these rules.

8. Privacy

Information you provide to enter is used only to administer the Raffle and is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. It is not sold or shared for marketing.

9. Not Sponsored by Third Parties

This Raffle is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Valley Studio, or any other third party. Valley Studio is the prize venue only.
